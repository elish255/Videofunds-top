
## Admin stage controls and completed-cycle routing
- Added CSRF-protected admin actions to remove Activation, Exchange, or Tax payment completion from a user profile.
- Removing Activation also clears Exchange and Tax completion because later stages depend on Activation. Removing Exchange also clears Tax. Removing Tax affects only Tax.
- Clearing a stage resets its paid timestamp/reference and sends the account back to that stage.
- After SplashPay confirms Tax, the user is sent to the dashboard instead of the withdrawal-processing screen.
- On the user's next attempt to start the withdrawal flow, a fully completed Activation/Exchange/Tax cycle is reset to a fresh Activation cycle. This avoids forcing the user to repeat Tax immediately after completing it and returning to the dashboard.
- Root and `public/` copies are kept in sync.
